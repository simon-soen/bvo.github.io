About Us

Bumala Vision Community Based Organization was founded in 2010 with a mission to transform lives in Bumala and surrounding communities through education, empowerment, and sustainable development.

Our organization was born out of the need to address the challenges facing vulnerable groups in our community, particularly children from poor families and young mothers who lacked support systems.

Our Mission

To uplift vulnerable communities through quality education, mentorship programs, and economic empowerment initiatives that promote self-reliance.

Our Vision

A thriving community where every individual has access to education, opportunities, and the support needed to reach their full potential.
